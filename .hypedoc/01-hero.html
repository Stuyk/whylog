whylog

Agents forget everything. whylog writes it down.

Every task leaves a session. Every rule keeps its reason. The next agent reads both before it touches anything.

$ whylog open "fix duplicate writes"
opened 9f3a
$ whylog note 9f3a "POST retried on 429"
$ whylog decide 9f3a \
"**No retries in the HTTP client**
 - *they caused duplicate writes*"
$ whylog close 9f3a --summary "no retries"
closed 9f3a - 1 file, 1 decision
